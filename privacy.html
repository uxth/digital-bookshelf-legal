Privacy Policy — Digital Bookshelf

Last updated: May 15, 2026

What we do

Digital Bookshelf ("we", "the app") is a publishing automation tool that helps independent authors post short-form book promotion videos to their own TikTok accounts.

What we collect

We only access the data that you explicitly authorize through TikTok's OAuth flow:

  • Your TikTok user profile (display name, avatar, follower count) — to identify which account to post to
  • Video upload permissions — to publish video files you have generated

We do not collect:

  • TikTok messages or comments
  • Other users' data
  • Browser history
  • Device location
  • Payment information (handled by Beacons / Payhip / Stripe)

How we use it

  • Authentication: verify you are the owner of the TikTok account
  • Posting: upload .mp4 files you have created to your TikTok feed
  • Logging: keep a local record of which videos were posted (for retry on failure)

Data storage

  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access_token, refresh_token) are stored locally on your own machine in tokens_<account>.json
  • We do not run a cloud server
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party
  • We do not use your data for advertising or machine learning training

Your rights

  • You can revoke our access at any time: https://www.tiktok.com/setting/security-and-privacy/connected-apps
  • You can delete the local token file at any time
  • You can request deletion of any data we hold by emailing the operator

Third parties

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking. The only third party we interact with is TikTok itself (via the official Content Posting API).

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect any changes.

Contact

For questions, open an issue at github.com/uxth/digital-bookshelf-legal/issues
