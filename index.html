About

Digital Bookshelf is a personal publishing automation pipeline that helps independent authors generate and post short-form book promotion videos to their TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and Pinterest accounts.

The operator is the sole user. No accounts are accepted from external users.
